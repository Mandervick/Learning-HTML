In this project, I created a mentoring website for students to get further guidance or support throughout their studies. This allows students to connect with others who may be on the same course as
them and know what they have been through. Students such as Emma Thompson and Jonathan Luke have a low income and may not be able to afford private tuition. Therefore, this mentoring page is cost effective
to ensure that it is accessible to all types of students. This meets the user stories as it provides a user-friendly platform to help get guidance and support for their studies with a low cost and
allows them to view the mentor's availability and start chatting to them or schedule a call. Students can also ask questions and get quick responses which can be a great help to students. The
website is accessible, responsive and scalable to all different types of screen sizes such as computers and phones.

Using the wireframes, I have stuck with the same consistent blue background. I also used the same navigation bar and footer across all my web-pages so it is consistent throughout from the wireframes
and created a sign up and log in form. I moved the logo to the left side of the page to make it more user-friendly as that is where most company logos are shown. I put a search bar on the right hand side
of the navigation bar as shown from the wireframes but this is not active. Every button is accessible via the keyboard to meet accessibility and usability requirements and to create a better user
experience. All button names and text match with real-world terms. For all images I put in my document, I ensured they comply with the rule of thirds and the golden ratio. I've made sure that the
sign up, log in and contact us pages do not have a lot of text to ensure only necessary content is shown which means they meet the signal to noise design principle. The text displayed on the screen
is clear and has a clear contrast compared to the background colour. All form labels are associated with their input fields for screen readers. I have also made sure that all interactive elements
and form fields are accessible via the keyboard. When a user doesn't fill in a field, a clear error message is displayed to the user telling them where exactly they need to input data which offers
the user immediate feedback of any errors. The "Remember me" checkbox is also keyboard-operable and screen-reader-friendly. I have made sure that the design elements such as the buttons, styles,
font sizes are consistent throughout the form. The user can easily undo or redo any actions they took by mistake. I have used images and iconic representation for the student to easily identify
what each button does. I have also used real world colours that are globally known on my buttons, for example, cancel is in red and the submit button is in green so that the user can make a
decision quicker on the button they should press. The home page is split into two columns and I've made sure that all other pages are aligned properly on all different screen sizes to meet
the user and the persona's needs for ease of use.

The home page is designed with information and images to draw people in and to make the page look more attractive. I've added clear buttons on the home page to easily go to the sign up page. The
user then needs to choose whether they want to sign up as a mentee or a mentor. They will have to enter their full name, email and create a password to sign up. If the user already has an account,
they can go to the log in page to sign in with their email and password. If any customer has any questions, they can always use the contact us page easily accessible on the navigation bar. To
contact us they should enter their full name, email and their message to us. For consistency, all the pages are created with a footer at the bottomn explaining that the page is copyrighted
and allows them to access our terms and conditions and privacy policy. The terms and conditions can also be accessed through the sign up form when the user must agree to them to continue. The
log in and sign up page both have a "remember me" checkbox to easily sign the user in if they are using a private computer and are happy to do so. This box is not ticked by default for security,
but the user has the choice to if they so wish.

During the creation of the website, I kept refreshing the pages I modified to make sure they did as I wanted. This made sure I tested the web-pages
after every edit I made. After finishing all the pages, I then tested the whole website, I made sure everything appeared as I wanted and made sure all the buttons worked as expected.
